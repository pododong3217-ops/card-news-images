Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

This policy covers the app "개발자의 게임픽" (Developer's Game Pick), a small automation tool that publishes content to Instagram accounts run by its owner, including @pixeltimemachine.builds, @dev.gamepic and @ai_podo_news, and to the Pixel Time Machine YouTube channel.

What the app does

  • Publishes posts, Reels and captions to the owner's own accounts.
  • Reads the comments left on the owner's own posts.
  • For the "Pixel Village" series: when someone comments on a village episode, their public username is added to a list of village residents, shown as a name tag in a later episode, and the app replies to welcome them.
  • Reads basic performance numbers (views, likes, saves) of the owner's own posts.

What data is used

Only information that is already public on the owner's posts: the commenter's username, the comment text and its time. The app does not access private messages of other people, contact details, passwords, payment details or location.

How it is stored and shared

Usernames of village residents are kept in a file on the owner's computer for as long as the series runs. The data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with anyone except as public name tags in the videos.

Removing your data

Comment "move out" on any Pixel Village episode and your username is removed from the village and will not be shown again. You can also send a direct message to @pixeltimemachine.builds on Instagram to ask for your data to be deleted. Requests are handled within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
